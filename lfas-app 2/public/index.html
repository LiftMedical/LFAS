Scoring & iPad home-screen guide

Blank means unscored and keeps a domain incomplete. A score of 0 means assessed and absent. N/A means deliberately not assessable and is excluded from the denominator. Every domain requires at least four signs scored 0–4, with every other sign marked N/A. A domain below that minimum shows “Insufficient assessable items.” Domain scores are means rounded to one decimal. Overall LFAS averages the four rounded domain scores and appears only when all four domains are complete.

For mixed patterns, all domains within 0.5 points of the highest rounded score share the leading group. No primary/secondary order is forced within that group. Outside a mixed pattern, the second-highest score is secondary; exact secondary ties are listed together. All-zero scores have no driver.

On iPad: open the deployed app in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. After the first online load, the app is available offline. Assessments stay only in this open page; refreshing or closing it clears your entries. Copy results before leaving. App files are cached for offline use; assessment entries are not.
